Frequently asked questions

How do I save my chat history?

History is saved automatically for logged-in users. Use the History page to review, rename, or archive conversations.

Can I use Nexxorra without an account?

Yes. Guest mode stores your workspace locally until you sign up or log in to sync it online.

Why am I seeing a Gemini API key error?

This means the backend Gemini configuration is missing or invalid. Contact support if the issue continues.

How do I reset my password?

Use the Forgot Password page to request a reset email. Then follow the secure link to update your password.
